# Surface brief: Cutframe site (home, style pages, make-your-own)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: creators arriving on a phone from a Reel or LinkedIn post. Action: copy a style's prompt (working builder on the style page, reachable from the first viewport), then follow the creator. Proof: the Paper Cut sample video, its 30 frames, the live title card. Constraints: static Astro + Preact; no invented testimonials or numbers; name "Cutframe" is a working name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Cutframe is a risograph zine print shop: every style is a printed issue and copying the prompt is pulling your own print. It refuses the dark AI-tool landing (centred headline, glow, feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: Two-ink riso on uncoated stock: fluorescent pink #FF48B0 and blue #0078BF overprinting (multiply), yellow #FFE800 as a third drum, paper #F4F0E6, ink black #1A1A1A. Halftone dot grain, slight misregistration, staples, fold and trim marks, issue numbers. Type: a heavy condensed grotesk display set huge and overprinted; a plain grotesk for text. Blue is the action ink by law; nothing decorative is blue-filled.

STORY: The visitor sees a real printed issue playing (the sample), understands one prompt makes it, picks the issue, types a topic on the order slip, copies the prompt, and follows the maker for the next issue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed pink print. CUTFRAME at about 20vw across the top, blue overprinted on pink with a 6px misregistered pink echo. Below-left, the Paper Cut sample playing in a stapled printed frame, rotated 1.5deg, about 46% width. Right: the one-line offer, "Animation styles for Claude. One prompt, one video. Free.", and a blue "Get Issue 01: Paper Cut" button. Issue strip at the foot: Issue 01 · Paper Cut · out now / Issue 02 · next month.

FORM: Riso zine print shop, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:4a350d48c394b1dd
Raises: from sd-seedbed-lobes, one ink owns the whole first viewport; from medium-native-crt-arcade-pixel-glow, blue is reserved for the action; from pop-culture-shelf-football-kit-crest, each style page reprints in its own ink pair with the masthead fixed.
Signature interaction: the print pull. Copying the prompt runs a short "print" motion: the order slip feeds through and a stamped print slides out with "Copied".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
